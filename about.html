About me

Backend minded.
AI curious.

Deep JadavAhmedabad, IN

An MCA graduate who learns by building.

I'm Deep Jadav, a software developer focused on backend development, modern web applications and AI-powered solutions. I work across the MERN stack — MongoDB, Express.js, React.js and Node.js — with most of my energy on the server side: REST API design, JWT authentication and database integration.

What started as curiosity about modern web technologies has grown into a habit of shipping complete projects: JWT-secured CRUD systems, a task manager, an e-commerce storefront and, more recently, AI applications — a RAG PDF chatbot, an AI-assisted deck analyser, an AI research & recommendation agent and LeadScope, an AI lead intelligence platform.

I'm especially interested in AI automation: using models like Gemini to turn slow, manual research into structured, source-backed results that people can act on.
